# Simulation Boundaries

## Non-claims

- No subjective-consciousness claim.
- No moral-patienthood claim.
- No autonomous language claim.
- No real science claim.
- No complete civilization simulator claim.
- No full physics engine claim.
- No production persistence claim.

## Dependency boundary

Resident behavior in the current foundation does not require LLM calls. The shell uses bounded local state, deterministic evaluators, seeded artifacts, source links, and replay/audit records.

## Technology boundary

There is no normal deterministic technology tree. Practices become historical nodes only after repeated resident behavior, usefulness, available materials, remembered evidence, failures, social transmission, and maintenance pressure.

## Reality-like causal boundary

The system should preserve these invariants:

- No effect without cause.
- No resource without source.
- No work without time, labor, material, or attention.
- No recovery without cost, care, time, or stabilization.
- No technology without observation, practice, materials, tools, failure, and transmission.
- No hidden simulator law as normal resident knowledge.
- No player omniscience in normal view.
- No avatar-installed correct concept.
- No permanent punishment loops.

## Audit boundary

Audit view may show hidden laws, source links, causal ledger entries, and simulator invariants. Normal resident view should show local names, observations, beliefs, visible behavior, and public consequences.
