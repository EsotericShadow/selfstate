# Report 234: SSRM-3D Pre-Avatar Society, Market, Ritual, Proto-Language Epoch Bridge

Status: pass

## Purpose

Report 234 moves from individual many-day ego continuity into long pre-avatar society scaffolding. The goal is to start building the condition described by the larger project: the avatar should enter only after a long simulated history in which households, markets, rituals, proto-language, technology, culture, and ecological pressures have already developed.

This is still deterministic scaffolding. It is not a real civilization, not subjective consciousness, not autonomous natural language emergence, and not a full playable world. It creates inspectable long-epoch state that a future local playable sandbox can use.

## New channels

Report 234 adds these testable channels:

- Pre-avatar epoch history across more than 4,000 simulated years.
- Avatar absence integrity: no avatar is present in the pre-entry trace.
- Avatar gate delay: gates remain blocked before civilization and minimum-year thresholds are satisfied.
- Mature gate readiness: the final mature epoch can become eligible while still recording avatar absence.
- Household continuity from the five ego-agent lineages into persistent social houses.
- Multi-agent market exchanges with fairness and reciprocity memory.
- Household rituals that bind body patterns, sounds, materials, and social functions.
- Proto-language tokens with roots, modifiers, grounded referents, compositional use, adoption, and stability.
- Technology lineages with precursors, innovations, maintenance cost, safety effect, market effect, and rollback plan.
- Cultural norms with protected values, enforcement style, and repair path.
- Sensory ecology packets binding visuals, sounds, smells, temperature, wetness, pain risk, comfort affordances, vibration rates, and body-cost notes.
- Flower/frequency epoch phases as timing scaffolding only.

## Honest interpretation

The first Report 234 run failed because `technology_lineage_depth` was only 0.548000. The initial metric scored primitive early epochs as if every epoch should already have mature technology. That was the wrong scoring model for a pre-avatar growth bridge. The corrected metric measures lineage completeness, primitive-to-mature improvement, and final maturity.

The pass means this report now has a compact long-history scaffold with markets, rituals, proto-language, technology, norms, sensory ecology, and avatar-entry gating. It does not mean the society is alive, autonomous, or subjectively aware.

## Avatar boundary

The avatar boundary is explicit:

- Before year 3000, the avatar gate remains blocked.
- At year 4181, civilization thresholds can be eligible.
- Even at eligibility, this report records `avatar_present = false` because this is still a pre-entry scaffold.
- A future report must implement the actual entry ceremony and playable avatar interaction after mature thresholds are satisfied.

## Moral and design boundary

This bridge does not create moral patienthood or real consent. It preserves the architecture boundary from earlier reports:

- distress-like signals remain bounded and recoverable
- repair paths are part of cultural norms
- ownership and refusal are functional simulation states
- proto-language is symbolic and grounded, not proof of inner speech
- sensory ecology is structured input, not felt experience
- no metaphysical claim is made from frequency or flower phases
