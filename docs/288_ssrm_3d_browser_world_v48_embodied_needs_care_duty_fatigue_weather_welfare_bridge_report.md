# Report 288: SSRM-3D Browser World v48 Embodied Needs/Care Duty/Fatigue Weather Welfare Bridge

## Purpose

Report 288 extends the browser-world line from resident social obligations into embodied welfare. The new benchmark gives residents body-state costs inside social schedules, household care duties, fatigue/rest negotiation, weather exposure during object loans, and recoverable welfare state that is visible without becoming a suffering spectacle.

This is deterministic browser-local scaffolding. It does not claim subjective consciousness, real consent, moral patienthood, autonomous natural language, or a complete 3D engine. The advance is that resident behavior now carries explicit body needs and welfare recovery paths rather than only social ledger state.

## Boundary

Deterministic browser-local embodied-need/care-duty/fatigue-rest/weather-welfare scaffold only; no LLM call, subjective consciousness, real consent, autonomous natural language, moral patienthood, complete gameplay, complete 3D engine, or metaphysical frequency claim.

## Method

The experiment builds from the v47 absence-negotiation/role-conflict/loan-default/forgiveness bridge and adds a deterministic v48 loop over 216 session days and 3888 browser ticks. Each tick samples a settlement, resident, partner, caregiver, witness, role, location, weather condition, and loan object. Resident body state tracks energy, fatigue, hunger, thirst, temperature, wetness, pain, comfort, safety, breath rate, movement effort, rest debt, and injury/degradation.

The new loop records eight evidence families: embodied need frames, social schedule need frames, household care duty frames, fatigue/rest negotiations, weather-exposure loan frames, recoverable welfare frames, welfare reload probes, and browser surface ticks. Frequency and flower-of-life fields are included only as deterministic timing/rate metadata for body and welfare loops, not as a metaphysical claim.

Distress is bounded by design. Negative states open care opportunities, shelter moves, rest negotiation, and visible recovery markers. The benchmark intentionally caps the `distress_not_spectacle` channel so a system cannot score perfectly by maximizing visible distress.

## Honest interpretation

Report 288 does not create subjective experience, real pain, real consent, autonomous language, or moral patienthood. It creates a deterministic browser-local evidence scaffold for body-state coupling and welfare recovery.

The important gain is that body needs now affect schedule behavior, care burden, rest negotiation, loan routing, and visible welfare state. The system cannot simply make residents busy or socially consistent; it must account for fatigue, wetness, temperature, pain markers, hunger/thirst pressure, recovery latency, care availability, and replayable memory. That is a necessary step toward convincing first-person artificial life, but it remains a functional simulation boundary rather than a consciousness claim.

The capped weakest channel is deliberate. If future work makes distress more visually intense without improving care/recovery, the benchmark should penalize it. The intended direction is recoverable welfare, not suffering-maximization.
