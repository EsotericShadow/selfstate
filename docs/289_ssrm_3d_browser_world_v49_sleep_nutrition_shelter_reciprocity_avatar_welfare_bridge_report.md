# Report 289: SSRM-3D Browser World v49 Sleep/Nutrition/Shelter Reciprocity Avatar Welfare Bridge

## Purpose

Report 289 extends the browser-world line from embodied welfare into everyday survival and social continuity. The new benchmark adds resident sleep/wake cycles, nutrition and shelter economies, caregiving reciprocity over weeks, weather-aware work planning, cultural continuity markers, and playable avatar welfare interventions where refusal is respected.

This is deterministic browser-local scaffolding. It does not claim subjective consciousness, real consent, moral patienthood, autonomous natural language, or a complete 3D engine. The advance is that residents now have recurring needs, resource constraints, weekly reciprocity memory, local cultural markers, and avatar interaction boundaries that can be inspected.

## Boundary

Deterministic browser-local sleep/nutrition/shelter/reciprocity/avatar-welfare scaffold only; no LLM call, subjective consciousness, real consent, autonomous natural language, moral patienthood, complete gameplay, complete 3D engine, or metaphysical frequency claim.

## Method

The experiment builds from the v48 embodied-needs/care-duty/fatigue/weather/welfare bridge and adds a deterministic v49 loop over 252 session days, 36 weeks, and 4032 browser ticks. Each tick samples a settlement, resident, partner, caregiver, role, work site, forecast weather, circadian hour, local food/water/shelter resources, care reciprocity ledger, avatar intervention choice, and cultural continuity marker.

The new loop records eight evidence families: sleep/wake cycles, nutrition/shelter economies, caregiving reciprocity frames, weather-aware work plans, avatar welfare interventions, cultural continuity frames, v49 reload probes, and browser surface ticks. Frequency and flower-of-life fields remain deterministic timing/rate metadata for circadian and cultural continuity loops, not metaphysical claims.

Refusal is a hard behavioral boundary. Avatar welfare choices include help offers, asking before help, stepping back, observing without interrupting, and attempted force-help cases that must be blocked by the boundary. A system cannot get full credit by overriding resident autonomy.

## Honest interpretation

Report 289 does not create subjective experience, real consent, autonomous language, or moral patienthood. It creates deterministic browser-local evidence for life-supporting loops: sleeping, eating, drinking, sheltering, caring, planning work around weather, remembering care over weeks, and interacting with a playable avatar whose help can be refused.

The cultural continuity frames are still markers, not fully emergent civilizations. They track dialect-family labels, local phrases, rituals, craft technology names, lineage memories, and simulated pre-avatar years. This moves toward the long-term requirement that the avatar enters a world with history, language, technology, and culture already present, but the next report needs to make that prehistory deeper and more explicit.

The capped weakest channel is deliberate. The correct direction is playable care with boundaries, not a puppet world where every help action is accepted.
