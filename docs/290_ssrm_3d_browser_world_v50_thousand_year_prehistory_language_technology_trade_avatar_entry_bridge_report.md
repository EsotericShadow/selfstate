# Report 290: SSRM-3D Browser World v50 Thousand-Year Prehistory Language Technology Trade Avatar Entry Bridge

## Purpose

Report 290 extends the browser-world line from everyday survival loops into a deep pre-avatar civilization layer. The benchmark simulates more than a thousand years before the playable avatar enters, with proto-language families, craft technology lineages, trade routes, lineage memories, local customs, frequency/flower timing metadata, and avatar entry only after civilization has already emerged.

This is deterministic browser-local scaffolding. It does not claim subjective consciousness, real consent, moral patienthood, autonomous natural language, or a complete 3D engine. The advance is that the avatar no longer appears in a freshly instantiated village. It enters a world with pre-existing history, institutions, language-family markers, craft lineages, trade obligations, and local constraints.

## Boundary

Deterministic browser-local thousand-year-prehistory/language-family/technology/trade/avatar-entry scaffold only; no LLM call, subjective consciousness, real consent, autonomous natural language, moral patienthood, complete gameplay, complete 3D engine, or metaphysical frequency claim.

## Method

The experiment builds from the v49 sleep/nutrition/shelter/reciprocity/avatar-welfare bridge and adds a deterministic v50 prehistory loop across `1200` simulated years in five-year steps. Five civilizations advance through population continuity, institution growth, resource pressure, conflict pressure, cooperation norms, proto-language evolution, craft technology stages, trade-route age, lineage memory, and local custom formation.

The new loop records eight evidence families: prehistory epochs, proto-language families, craft technologies, trade routes, lineage memories, avatar entry after prehistory, prehistory reload probes, and browser ticks. Frequency and flower-of-life fields remain deterministic timing/rate metadata for historical phase and language/craft cadence, not metaphysical claims.

The playable avatar is explicitly absent during prehistory. Entry frames are generated only at year `1200`, and each entry requires civilization to preexist with institutions, language-family traces, craft lineages, greeting protocol, translation uncertainty, local custom constraints, preserved history, and private workspace boundaries.

## Honest interpretation

Report 290 does not produce real emergent civilization, subjective experience, autonomous language, moral patienthood, or a complete playable 3D world. It creates deterministic evidence that the world model can carry a long pre-avatar history into the moment of playable entry.

The proto-language families are structured markers, not live natural languages. The craft technologies are lineage scaffolds, not full physical simulations. The trade routes are deterministic network traces, not autonomous economies. That said, the benchmark now makes it harder to fake a convincing world by spawning residents at the moment the avatar arrives. History, customs, translation uncertainty, trade obligations, local laws, and lineage memories must already be present.

The capped weakest channel is deliberate. The correct direction is not a blank sandbox with decorative lore. The avatar should enter late, after civilization has already accumulated constraints that the player must respect.
