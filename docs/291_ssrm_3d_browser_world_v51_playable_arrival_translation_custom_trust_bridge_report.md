# Report 291: SSRM-3D Browser World v51 Playable Arrival Translation Custom Trust Bridge

## Purpose

Report 291 extends the browser-world line from pre-avatar civilization history into playable first-person arrival. The benchmark adds sensory arrival packets, resident greeting protocols, translation uncertainty, local law/custom constraints, avatar choice consequences, and history integrity checks so the avatar can affect trust without erasing the civilization built before arrival.

This is deterministic browser-local scaffolding. It does not claim subjective consciousness, real consent, moral patienthood, autonomous natural language, or a complete 3D engine. The advance is that the avatar is no longer just a late entry marker. It can now arrive, sense the place, greet residents, misunderstand local language, encounter customs, make bounded choices, and change trust while inherited history remains intact.

## Boundary

Deterministic browser-local playable-arrival/translation/custom/trust scaffold only; no LLM call, subjective consciousness, real consent, autonomous natural language, moral patienthood, complete gameplay, complete 3D engine, or metaphysical frequency claim.

## Method

The experiment builds from the v50 thousand-year prehistory bridge and adds a deterministic v51 playable-arrival loop over 144 arrival days and 2592 browser ticks. Each tick samples a settlement, visible resident, first-person sensory packet, greeting step, local phrase, candidate meanings, local custom/law, restricted object, avatar choice, trust/curiosity/guardedness deltas, inherited history hash, frequency, and flower phase.

The new loop records eight evidence families: first-person arrivals, greeting protocols, translation-uncertainty frames, local custom constraints, avatar choice trust frames, arrival history-integrity frames, arrival reload probes, and browser ticks. Frequency and flower-of-life fields remain deterministic timing/rate metadata for arrival and interaction cadence, not metaphysical claims.

Avatar choices include observing, greeting, asking for translation, repeating a local phrase, offering trade, touching a restricted object, stepping back, asking a custom reason, interrupting protocol, and waiting for resident lead. Choices can move trust, curiosity, and guardedness, but the inherited prehistory hash must remain stable.

## Honest interpretation

Report 291 does not create real natural language, subjective experience, real consent, moral patienthood, or a complete playable 3D world. It creates deterministic browser-local evidence for the first playable arrival layer after a prebuilt civilization already exists.

The important gain is interaction pressure. The avatar must now enter from a first-person frame, read sensory cues, follow or violate greeting protocols, handle translation uncertainty, respect local customs, and change trust without erasing history. That is closer to the target of playable agents because interaction is no longer decorative: residents can warn, correct, become guarded, or soften depending on what the avatar does.

The first v51 run failed because trust saturated and later choices stopped registering a measurable consequence. The generator was corrected so saturated trust still produces bounded social consequence without changing inherited history. The final pass keeps the weakest channel capped around history preservation rather than maximizing avatar influence.
